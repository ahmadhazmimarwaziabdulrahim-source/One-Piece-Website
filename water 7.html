Water 7 stands as one of the most visually captivating locations in One Piece, a city built entirely around water where canals replace streets and boats replace carriages. Its design, inspired by Venice, gives it a timeless charm, with layered buildings, arching bridges, and constant movement through flowing waterways.


The city feels alive, shaped by both nature and human ingenuity, and its identity revolves around the sea. Shipbuilding is not just an industry here but a tradition passed down through generations, making Water 7 a place where craftsmanship is deeply respected and celebrated.


Water 7, known as the "Capital of Water" (水の都, Mizu no Miyako?), is a city in Paradise known for its shipwrights.[1] It is the main setting of the Water 7 Arc and Post-Enies Lobby Arc. The Straw Hat Pirates stopped here to trade in their gold for belly, as well as to repair the Going Merry and also to find a new shipwright for their crew. It is part of the route their log was on, heading there directly after Long Ring Long Land, making it the eighth island in Paradise that they visited.

Water 7 ultimately represents change and growth, both for the city and for the Straw Hat Pirates. After facing internal conflict and external threats, the crew emerges stronger, with a deeper understanding of each other and their shared goals. The loss of the Going Merry marks the end of an era, but it also paves the way for a new beginning. This transition highlights one of the core themes of One Piece, moving forward even when it means letting go of something precious.


The city itself mirrors this idea of resilience. Constantly threatened by Aqua Laguna, Water 7 survives through adaptation and determination, with its people rebuilding time and time again.


It is also where Franky, a skilled shipwright with a unique personality, becomes an important part of the crew's future. His contribution to building a new ship symbolizes hope and continuity, ensuring that while the past is honored, the journey ahead remains full of possibility.
