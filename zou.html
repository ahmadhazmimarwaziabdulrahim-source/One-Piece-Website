Zou is one of the most unusual and mysterious locations in One Piece, unlike any traditional island in the Grand Line. Instead of being fixed in the sea, it exists on the back of a massive, ancient elephant named Zunesha, constantly walking through the New World. The island itself is covered in lush forests, waterfalls, and a unique ecosystem that thrives atop the moving creature. This gives Zou a sense of isolation and wonder, making it feel almost hidden from the rest of the world.


Zou is an island located on the back of a Naitamie-Norida elephant named Zunesha that roams the New World. It is known as a "Phantom Island" (幻の島, Maboroshi no Shima?) due to Log Poses being unable to point to it. It is home to the Mokomo Dukedom of the Mink Tribe as well as where the Heart Pirates resided while waiting for their captain to return from Dressrosa.

Beyond its strange nature, Zou is defined by its inhabitants, the Mink Tribe. These animal-like humanoids live in harmony with nature and have their own strong culture and traditions. The Mokomo Dukedom is divided into two main rulers, representing day and night, showing the balance that exists within their society. Despite their peaceful way of life, the Minks are fierce warriors when their home is threatened.


When the Straw Hat Pirates arrive, they discover that Zou has recently suffered a devastating attack. The Beasts Pirates, led by Jack, left destruction across the island while searching for Raizo. Even after such suffering, the Minks refuse to betray their ally, showing incredible loyalty and unity. This moment highlights one of the strongest themes of the arc, the importance of trust and honor.

Zou plays a crucial role in revealing deeper secrets of the world. It is here that the concept of Road Ponegylphs is introduced, which are essential for reaching the final island, Laugh Tale. This discovery shifts the story forward, connecting the Straw Hats to the larger mystery of the One Piece and the true history of the world.


The arc also strengthens alliances between the Straw Hats, the Minks, and the Heart Pirates. Plans are set into motion for future conflicts, especially against powerful enemies in the New World. Zou may not have large battles like other arcs, but its importance lies in the information it reveals and the bonds it strengthens, making it a key turning point in the journey ahead.
