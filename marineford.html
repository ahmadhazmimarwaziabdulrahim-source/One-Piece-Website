Marineford is one of the most important and heavily guarded locations in One Piece, serving as the former headquarters of the Marines. The island is built as a massive military stronghold, designed for defense and authority rather than beauty. Its wide plaza, towering walls, and strategic layout reflect its purpose as the center of Marine power. Unlike other islands in the Grand Line, Marineford represents order and control, standing in direct opposition to piracy.


Marineford was the island of the base of operations for Marine Headquarters, where now the Marine Base G-1 is situated. It is where former Fleet Admiral Sengoku and the Admirals resided, along with many Vice Admirals and lower officers when they were not assigned to another base. The city surrounding Marineford was inhabited by the families of Marine soldiers.

Beyond its role as a headquarters, Marineford is deeply tied to the balance of power in the world. It is located in a highly strategic position, making it a key point for both defense and control over the seas. The island's connection to the World Government further strengthens its importance, placing it at the center of global authority and military action.


It is situated near the Sabaody Archipelago and directly next to Mary Geoise on the Red Line. The only safe way to reach Marineford is through the Gates of Justice. However, after the Summit War of Marineford, the location of the Marine Headquarters base of operations was exchanged with that of Marine Base G-1, which was subsequently renamed New Marineford.

Marineford is best known as the setting of the Paramount War, one of the most intense and emotional conflicts in the series. This battle brought together some of the strongest figures in the world, including Marines, Warlords, and the forces of Whitebeard. The war was not only a clash of power but also a turning point that reshaped the world and its future.


The aftermath of the war left a lasting impact on both the Marines and the pirates. It marked the end of an era and signaled the rise of a new generation. Marineford, once a symbol of absolute authority, became a reminder of the cost of war and the shifting balance between justice and freedom in the world of One Piece.
