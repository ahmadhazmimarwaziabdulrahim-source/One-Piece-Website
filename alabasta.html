Alabasta Kingdom (also known as the Arabasta Kingdom) is a desert nation located in Paradise on the Grand Line. It is ruled by the Nefertari Family, one of the twenty founding families of the World Government. Unlike the others, the Nefertari family chose to remain in their homeland instead of moving to Mary Geoise, giving the kingdom a unique historical position.


The land is dominated by vast deserts, extreme heat, and scarce water resources. Despite this, Alabasta has developed major cities such as the capital Alubarna.

Alabasta became the center of a major crisis when a civil war broke out between the royal army and rebel forces. This conflict was secretly orchestrated by the criminal organization Baroque Works, led by the Warlord Crocodile. By manipulating water supplies and spreading misinformation, Crocodile pushed the kingdom toward collapse in order to seize power and uncover Pluton.


Princess Nefertari Vivi discovered the truth and sought help from the Straw Hat Pirates. Together, they worked to stop the war and reveal the conspiracy. Monkey D. Luffy eventually defeated Crocodile, exposing his plans and ending the conflict before the kingdom could be destroyed.

Alabasta was the fourth island visited by the Straw Hat Pirates on the Grand Line, and the events there greatly increased their notoriety. Following the battle, Luffy's bounty rose to Beli 100,000,000, while Roronoa Zoro received a bounty of Beli 60,000,000.


After peace was restored, King Nefertari Cobra continued to rule the kingdom, and Vivi chose to remain behind to support her people. Alabasta stands as a symbol of resilience, loyalty, and unity, and is considered one of the most important arcs in the series due to its emotional depth and long lasting impact on the story.
